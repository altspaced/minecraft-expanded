## Minecraft Expanded
Minecraft Expanded is a carefully curated Vanilla+ modpack that enhances performance, visuals, and immersion while staying true to the core Minecraft experience.<br>

This pack expands the world with rich new biomes, beautiful structures, unique mobs, and quality-of-life improvements — all without breaking the familiar feel of vanilla. Whether you're exploring, building, or fighting, everything feels like a natural extension of the base game.
## Features:

**Major Performance Boost —** Built with Sodium, Lithium, Krypton, ModernFix, ImmediatelyFast and more for smooth gameplay even with hundreds of mods.<br>

**Stunning Visuals & Immersion —** Iris shader support, enhanced animations, Sound Physics Remastered, dynamic lighting, and beautiful particle effects.<br>

**Massive World Expansion —** Terralith + Regions Unexplored for incredible new biomes, plus dozens of new structures from YUNG’s, Luki’s, Epic Structures, and more.<br>

**New Life & Wildlife —** Alex’s Mobs, Enderman Overhaul, Creeper Overhaul, Ribbits, and many other creature additions.<br>

**Quality of Life Done Right —** Trinkets, Farmer’s Delight + Chef’s Delight, Inventory Management, Better Combat, Xaero’s World Map, and dozens of small but impactful improvements.<br>

**Expanded Building & Decoration —** Macaw’s furniture series, Chipped, and many more decorative blocks.
